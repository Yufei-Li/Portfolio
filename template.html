How to use this

Copy any block below into your page. Everything marked TEMPLATE ANNOTATION in the stylesheet, plus every .tpl-label, .tpl-note and .tpl-spec, is scaffolding: delete it. The rest is the system.

Two readers. Aim for a five-second scan and a two-minute read. Keep the deck, the results strip and every section lead in the default view; push method, rationale and detail into a disclosure. Roughly 400 to 450 visible words is two minutes.

Measure length. Total visible words divided by 240 gives reading seconds. Add about 45 seconds for looking at visuals.

Cards

.info-cards stacks full width by default. Add .is-two for two across, but only when both cards hold a similar amount of text: grid equalises their height, so a short one next to a long one fills with dead space.

Two across

For a pair of comparable points, like what changed for one audience versus another.

Same weight

Keep the two bodies within a line or two of each other, or stack them instead.

Stacked, full width

For unequal content. A card holding three explained points sits badly beside one holding two bullets, so give each its own row and let it size to what it contains.

Card grid, clickable

A 2×N grid of numbered cards. Make a card a <button> with data-open-screens="id" and it opens the matching dialog; you get Enter, focus rings and aria-haspopup for free. Cards without a dialog stay as <div>.

Galleries

Data

Three ways to show numbers, all built from CSS variables so they invert cleanly and stay crisp. Never screenshot a chart: at 560px wide, a 2880px export renders its text at about 4px.

two-series comparison

Dark is the treatment group throughout, light is the control. Keep the colour meaning the series, not which result is better, so the eye can compare across metrics. Each metric normalises to its own larger value, which lets a count sit beside two percentages.

Media

Three figure widths. Body figures sit at the measure. .wide-shot is for dense screenshots and can be linked to open full size. .device wraps a shot in a laptop frame for a hero. Placeholders below stand in for real images.

Before publishing

  • Every link resolves. No href="#" placeholders left.
  • Every image has alt text describing what it shows, not its filename.
  • The five-second view carries a result, not just a title and a picture.
  • Visible words divided by 240, plus 45 seconds, comes to about two minutes.
  • No em dashes or en dashes.
  • Nothing scrolls sideways at 375px, and no dialog scrolls at 700px tall.
  • Numbers are stated with their n, and caveats sit next to the claims they qualify.
