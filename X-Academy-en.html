Overview

X Academy is a 2-week tech summer camp primarily for international high school students, serving ~300 students annually with an average class size of ~25. As a Teaching Assistant for Intro to Data Science (2023) and Natural Language Processing (2024), I co-developed course curricula, designed and facilitated Lab Hours, and mentored students through their capstone projects. As Extracurricular Activities Organizer in 2025, I organized camp-wide activities and served as a primary student coordinator.

Teaching

To help students better grasp computer science concepts, I designed lab exercises tailored to their interests, making abstract theories and backend code easier to understand.

01 · Intro to Data Science Lab
  • Overview: A 90-minute hands-on data science lab where students step into the shoes of a student union president to clean, integrate, deduce, and transform messy real-world campus crowdfunding data.
  • Core Tech Stack: Python Jupyter Notebook Pandas Data Cleaning
  • Features:
    • Contextual Learning: Utilizes a relatable campus charity drive dataset containing 200+ messy records with currency text, negative outliers, whitespace keys, and missing grades.
    • Cognitive Framework Construction: Installs a 4-stage mental model (Cleaning, Integration, Deduction, Transformation), equipping students to intuitively audit raw datasets and know which operations to execute in future data projects.

02 · NLP: Language Models & Perplexity Lab
  • Overview: A 90-minute hands-on NLP lab where high school students analyze pre-built N-gram language model infrastructure and complete the Perplexity evaluation engine to assess language model uncertainty.
  • Core Tech Stack: Python Jupyter Notebook N-Gram Modeling Perplexity
  • Features:
    • Read-Comprehend-Complete Scaffolding: Pre-implemented data preprocessing, tokenization, and N-gram counting modules; offloaded syntax overhead so zero-background students could focus entirely on reading, comprehending, and completing the core evaluation logic.
    • Flowchart-Guided Conceptual Mapping: Utilized visual flowcharts to diagram the end-to-end data pipeline (Text → Tokens → N-grams → Probabilities → Perplexity), helping students visually trace execution logic and map mathematical formulas (Equation 4.1) directly to Python for loops.
